## 5. Accessibility Service

To deliver context-aware reminders, the App requests Android's Accessibility Service permission. By enabling this permission, you acknowledge and agree that:

- The App will read on-screen text and notification content from your device solely to detect Islamic keywords.
- This processing occurs locally on your device. Raw text is never stored or transmitted.
- You can revoke this permission at any time via **Settings > Accessibility > Hayah**.
- Disabling this permission will prevent the App from delivering automatic context-aware reminders, but all other features remain available.
