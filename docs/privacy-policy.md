## 1. Overview

Hayah is a context-aware Quranic reminder app. It detects meaningful moments in your day and surfaces a relevant Quranic verse via notification. This policy explains what data the app accesses, what it stores, and how it is used.

---

## 2. Data Accessed via Accessibility Service

Hayah uses Android's Accessibility Service to read on-screen text and notification content from your device. This access is used **solely** to detect Islamic keywords (e.g., "patience", "gratitude", "stress") that may indicate a meaningful moment worth a Quranic reminder.

**What is read:**
- On-screen text from apps you have open
- Notification text and content descriptions

**How it is processed:**
- Text is scanned **locally on your device** in memory.
- The raw text is **never stored** and **never transmitted** to any server.
- Only the matched keyword (a single word or short phrase such as "patience") is retained.

**What is NOT done with this data:**
- Hayah does not record, log, or upload your screen content.
- Hayah does not read passwords, payment information, or secure text fields.
- Hayah does not share anything read via the Accessibility Service with any third party.

You can disable the Accessibility Service at any time via **Settings > Accessibility > Hayah**.

---

## 3. Data Stored on Your Device

All data generated by Hayah is stored **locally on your device** in a private Room database and Android DataStore. This data is never uploaded to Hayah's servers.

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Journal entries (surah/verse, keyword tag, reflection, translation, audio URL, page number, timestamp) | Your personal Quranic reminder history |
| Keyword cache (verse keys and AI-generated reflections per keyword) | Avoids regenerating content for the same keyword repeatedly |
| Read history (Quran page numbers you have visited in the app) | Powers the reading progress ring on the Home screen |
| User settings (custom keywords, reciter preference, detection thresholds, theme, audio toggle) | Your personalization choices |
| OAuth tokens (access token and refresh token) | Keeps you signed in to your Quran Foundation account |

---

## 4. Account Data (Quran Foundation)

Hayah uses **Quran Foundation OAuth** for sign-in. When you log in:

- You are redirected to Quran Foundation's login page (`oauth2.quran.foundation`).
- Hayah receives an access token and a refresh token. These are managed by the AppAuth library and stored privately on your device.
- Hayah requests your **public profile** (display name and profile picture) to personalize the Home screen greeting.
- Your Quran reading activity (pages read per day) is fetched from the Quran Foundation API to display your progress.

Hayah does not have access to your Quran Foundation password. Your account data is governed by [Quran Foundation's Privacy Policy](https://quran.com/privacy).

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

The following third-party services receive data from Hayah. **None of these services receive raw screen content or notification content.**

| Service | Data sent | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Quran Foundation API (`apis.quran.foundation`) | OAuth access token, verse keys | Fetch verse text, translations, tafsir, audio URLs, reading activity |
| Quran Foundation OAuth (`oauth2.quran.foundation`) | Login credentials (handled directly by Quran Foundation) | Authentication |
| Google Gemini (via Firebase AI SDK) | Matched keyword, verse translations and tafsir excerpts | Generate contextual verse reflections |
| Quran MCP Server (`mcp.quran.ai`) | Matched keyword | Search for relevant verse keys |
| Google Play Services | Activity type subscription | Detect physical activity (walking, driving) to trigger contextual reminders |

---

## 6. Physical Activity Recognition

Hayah requests the **Activity Recognition** permission (`ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION`) to detect when you are walking, running, cycling, or in a vehicle. When a transition is detected, a matching keyword (e.g., "walking", "travel") is processed through the same local reminder pipeline described above. No location data is accessed or stored.

---

## 7. Notifications

Hayah posts local notifications to deliver Quranic verse reminders. Notification content (the verse and reflection) is generated on-device. Hayah does not use push notification services.

---

## 8. Data Sharing

Hayah does **not** sell, trade, or rent your personal data to any third party. The only data that leaves your device is what is necessary to communicate with the third-party services listed in Section 5, as described there.

---

## 9. Data Retention

All data stored by Hayah (journal entries, keyword cache, settings) remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear the app's data via Android Settings. You can also delete individual journal entries from within the app.

---

## 10. Children's Privacy

Hayah is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

---

## 11. Your Rights

- **Disable Accessibility Service:** Settings > Accessibility > Hayah > turn off
- **Revoke Activity Recognition:** Settings > Apps > Hayah > Permissions
- **Delete all app data:** Settings > Apps > Hayah > Clear Data
- **Sign out:** removes your OAuth tokens from the device
